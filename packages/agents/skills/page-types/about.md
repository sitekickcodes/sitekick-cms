Typical order: hero that says who you are in one line; the story or reason you exist (short, specific, no mission-statement fog); what you believe or how you work, as three or four points; the people, with real names and roles only; proof (years, numbers, a quote); a gentle call to action (visit, get in touch, see the work).

Real details make an about page: a founding year, a place, a person. Never invent them; use placeholders.
