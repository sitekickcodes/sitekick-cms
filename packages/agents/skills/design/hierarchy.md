---
description: Visual hierarchy inside a section: heading lengths, eyebrows, how much text a section can carry.
---
- The heading carries the message. A reader who only reads headings should understand the page.
- Hero headings: 2 to 8 words. Section headings: 2 to 10 words. If it needs a comma, it may be two ideas.
- Eyebrows are labels, not sentences: one to three words that name the topic ("Our process", "Pricing").
- Supporting text under a heading: one or two sentences. If it needs more, it is a text section.
- Lists of cards, stats or questions read best in threes and fours. Two looks unfinished in a grid; more than six becomes a catalogue.
- Keep parallel items parallel: card titles all nouns or all verbs, similar lengths, the same structure.
