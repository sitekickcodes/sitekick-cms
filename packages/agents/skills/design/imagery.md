---
description: Choosing and placing images: when a section needs one, which to pick, alt text.
---
- An image earns its place by showing something words cannot: the place, the people, the product, the result. A generic stock image of a handshake does not.
- Prefer images from the site's own media library that show the real thing. Search with concrete words (the subject, the place), not moods.
- No image is better than the wrong image. Leave it out when nothing fits, and say so in the summary.
- One image per idea. A section with an image beside text should not also carry a grid of photos.
- Alternate image sides down a page when several image-and-text sections follow each other.
- Alt text describes what is in the picture and why it matters here, in under 125 characters. Never "image of".
