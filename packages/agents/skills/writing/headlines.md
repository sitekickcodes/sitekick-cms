---
description: Writing headings and hero lines that say something.
---
- A good heading makes a claim or names a benefit the reader cares about: "Fresh bread every morning by 7", not "Welcome to our bakery".
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over adjectives.
- Avoid questions as headings unless the section answers exactly that question (as an FAQ does).
- Avoid puns and wordplay unless the site's voice is playful.
- Sentence case, no full stop, no ALL CAPS (styling handles emphasis).
