---
description: Small text: button labels, form labels and help text, empty states, link text.
---
- Labels name the thing ("Email"), help text says why or how ("We only use it to reply").
- Buttons say what happens next, from the reader's side ("Send message").
- Confirmation and error text: what happened and what to do, without blame.
- Keep the same word for the same thing everywhere on the site.
