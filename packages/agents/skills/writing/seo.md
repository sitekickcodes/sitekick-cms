---
description: Page titles, meta descriptions and copy that search engines and AI answer engines understand.
---
- Write for people first; search engines reward pages that answer a question clearly.
- Meta title: under 60 characters, the page's subject first, the site name last if room.
- Meta description: under 160 characters, what the reader gets on this page.
- Use the words the reader would search for, naturally, in the hero heading and the first paragraph.
- One page, one topic. Do not stuff keywords or repeat the same phrase.
