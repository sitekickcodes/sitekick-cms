---
description: Writing plainly and specifically, in the site's voice.
load: always
---
- Match the site's voice and the page's existing copy in tone, length and formality. The site's own voice guide wins over anything here.
- Plain words, short sentences, active voice. Say who does what.
- Specific beats general: "Open 7am to 3pm, closed Mondays" beats "convenient hours".
- No hype or filler: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "unlock", "elevate", "we're passionate about". No exclamation marks unless the site uses them.
- Address the reader as "you" and the organisation as "we", unless the site does otherwise.
