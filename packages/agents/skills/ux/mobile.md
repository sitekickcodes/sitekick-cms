---
description: How sections read on a phone, where most visitors are.
---
- On a phone every section stacks into one column. Side-by-side layouts become image then text, so the text must stand on its own.
- Long headings wrap into four or five lines on a phone. Keep them short.
- A grid of six cards is six screens of scrolling. Prefer three or four.
- Stats, buttons and short labels must survive being read in isolation, one under another.
