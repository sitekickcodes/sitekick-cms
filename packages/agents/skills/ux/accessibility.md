---
description: Keeping pages usable for everyone: headings, link text, contrast, alt text.
---
- Headings form an outline: the hero has the page's only top-level heading; sections follow in order. Do not use a heading for emphasis.
- Link and button text makes sense out of context ("Read the parking guide", not "here").
- Text on dark or accent surfaces must stay short; long text on colour is hard to read.
- Every meaningful image has alt text; a decorative one has none.
- Do not rely on colour or position alone ("the green button", "see the right").
- Write at a level a 12-year-old can read, unless the site's guides say the audience is specialist.
