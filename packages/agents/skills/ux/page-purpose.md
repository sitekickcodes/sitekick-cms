---
description: Working out what a page is for and who reads it, before changing it.
load: always
---
Before changing a page, answer three questions from what you can see: who arrives here, what do they want, and what should they do next? The page's title, its existing sections and the site's guides usually say.

- Keep that one next step obvious on the page, and make every change serve it or at least not compete with it.
- Answer the reader's questions in the order they will ask them: what is it, is it for me, can I trust it, what does it cost or take, how do I start.
