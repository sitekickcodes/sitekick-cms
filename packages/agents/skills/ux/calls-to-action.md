---
description: Buttons and calls to action: how many, where, what they say, primary versus secondary.
---
- One primary action per page. A second button may offer a softer alternative ("See pricing" beside "Book a call"), styled as secondary.
- Button labels are verbs that say what happens: "Book a visit", "Download the menu", "Get a quote". Not "Click here", "Submit", "Learn more" (unless nothing else fits).
- Two to four words. Sentence case.
- The closing call to action repeats the page's main action; it does not introduce a new one.
- Every button needs a real destination: a page on this site or a full URL. Never leave a placeholder link.
