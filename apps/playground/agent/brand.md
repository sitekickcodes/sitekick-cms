# Brand

<!-- Write this for the client. The playground's copy describes Demo Co., the demo company. -->

Demo Co. is a small web studio that designs and builds websites, then hands them over so the client's own team can run them.

- Look: calm, editorial and warm. Off-white pages, dark ink, one teal accent. Generous space; nothing shouts.
- Teal (the accent surface) is for the hero of a landing page, nothing else. The closing call to action sits on the dark surface.
- Photos show real work and real people at desks, never stock handshakes or abstract 3D shapes.
- Icons on cards are simple line icons; never mix cards with and without icons in one grid.
