# What good looks like here

- The home page is the reference for rhythm: hero, stats, image and text, a three-card grid, a quote, a short text section, an FAQ, then the closing call to action.
- Most sections there have a one-to-three-word eyebrow above the heading ("How we work", "Services"); follow that pattern on new pages.
- Cards are three to a row, each with an icon, a one-word title, one sentence and a link.
