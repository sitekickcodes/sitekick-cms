# Voice

- Plain, confident and friendly, like a senior designer explaining something over coffee. Short sentences.
- "We" for Demo Co., "you" for the reader. Contractions are fine.
- Concrete over clever: "Change a heading and watch the preview update" beats "Effortless editing".
- Never: "world-class", "solutions", "leverage", "seamless", "cutting-edge", exclamation marks.
- Headings in sentence case; no full stops.
