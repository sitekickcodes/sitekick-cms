# Audience

- Who: founders, marketing leads and operations people at small organisations (5 to 200 staff): nonprofits, studios, local businesses.
- What they want: a site that looks professional and that their own team can update without calling a developer.
- What worries them: being locked in, surprise costs, a site that breaks when someone edits it.
- What we want them to do: start a project (the Contact page). Secondary: email us.
